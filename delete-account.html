Delete your 45 Hard account

45 Hard is an app by Hasp Labs LLC. You can delete your account, and everything in it, at any time.

Without the app

Email contact@hasplabs.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm. If you used Sign in with Apple and hid your email, include your 45 Hard handle; we will reply with one question to check the account is yours.

In the app

Open Profile, then Settings, then Account, then Delete account. The account is deleted at once.

What is deleted

Your profile, your challenges, daily logs and step totals, progress photos, profile photo, answers to "What got in the way?", friendships, cheers, sharing settings and push tokens. A challenge you started that other people are still running is handed to one of them, so their board keeps going; everything of yours inside it is deleted.

What is kept, and for how long

Your handle, and any handle you used before it, alone, for 30 days, so nobody else can take it right away. Encrypted backups, for up to 14 days. Our email provider's log of emails sent to you, for 30 days. A report you sent or that was made about you, and a feature request you sent: each keeps the handle it was sent under and is no longer linked to your account.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
