Keep every goal for 45 days

Pick the goals you keep putting off, do every one every day for 45 days, and finish with proof you can keep a promise to yourself.

Goals you choose

Water, a workout, reading, or anything you name. You set the targets, so each day asks for exactly what you decided mattered.

Miss one, start over

A missed goal sends you back to day 1. That is what makes a long run hard to give up, and day 45 worth having.

Company when you want it

Run it alone, or send one link and friends join the same challenge. Seeing who's still in makes it harder to be the one who drops out.
